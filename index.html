Type as Clay

Three experiments in shaping letterforms with your hands, tracked by the webcam. Each one also works with a mouse or trackpad. Allow camera access when asked.

  1. AAxis SculptorYour hands push Roboto Flex around its own design space: width, weight, slant, optical size and the parametric axes.
  2. BOutline WarpPinch the outline and pull. Contours behave like liquify, and the shape stays where you leave it.
  3. CSoft BodyLetters as physical material on a floor. Squeeze, swipe and press them. Plasticity sets rubber or clay.
